## Latest native diagnosis and historical recheck

Raw HTTP/2 frame observations reproduce the same 63-byte UDP truncation on
macOS 15.8.2 and 27.0.1. The native sender's frame declares 66 bytes with flags
0x0, although its Capsule header requires 67 bytes. The final payload byte is
already absent before server DATA parsing, padding removal or request-body
buffering. This explains the recorded continuous-stream desynchronization and
inner-HTTP/3 upload stalls. Independent Go clients pass 42 uploads of 64 MiB.
The server cannot safely repair this by guessing bytes or resynchronizing.
Current local Rust and race-enabled Caddy regressions preserve complete-message
delivery, legal varint widths and unknown-Capsule skipping. See the paired
network-relay report `docs/native-relay-capsule-boundary-2026-11-13.md` and
`/workspace/scratch/macos-resume/udp-raw-macos15-analysis.json` / the macos27
counterpart for exact payloads, run identifiers and artifact hashes.

and inline-review-thread lists have been retrieved. The ledger has 19 entries;

These counters measure allocator bytes rather than process RSS. Exact raw
counters are in `caddy-auth-before.log` and `caddy-auth-after.log`.
Absolute timing samples were collected on a shared
four-CPU runner under compilation load and are not claimed as WAN speedups.
Stable allocation and connection/error counts carry the conclusions.

Other factors remain workload dependent: receive-window/RTT and TCP buffer
limits, connection reuse, PMTU discovery, nested QUIC congestion behavior,
DNS setup, password-hash concurrency, bounded logging, per-user rate limits,
destination path quality and queue pressure. No default buffer, fragmentation,
security check or congestion behavior was relaxed simply to increase a number.

  transport behavior under their stated conditions. They cannot prove all
  regressions absent or certify an untested deployment.

  memory. Small deployments and aggregate client queues still require measured
  capacity planning.
